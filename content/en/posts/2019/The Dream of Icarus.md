No, it wasn't just a single time

That was merely the final one

Of all the attempts in his climb

Gravity proved strictly done
\
\
\
On his skin, the heat he wished to feel

And Victory to him wished to show

That if an altar to oneself one would reveal

Many others could be inspired to grow
\
\
\
He didn't give up, launched forth, insisted

On a journey that seemed selfish to the eye

In truth, for everyone he persisted

Even when fatigue made him suffer and sigh
\
\
\
Yes, it wasn't for himself that he flew

It was empathy that led him away

Nor was it the Sun that overthrew

But only that the ground came to stay
\
\
\
Hephaestus took from the fire

Proteus dried up the seas

From the wings, the bond did expire

And Icarus' Dream ceased to be.
